How to play
  • Name the country from its flag, its shape, or where it lights up on the globe.
  • Name the capital. A soft blob shows roughly where it sits.
  • Locate it by tapping the spinning globe as close as you can. Pinch, scroll, or use the +/− buttons to zoom in on small countries.
  • Tap a flag or shape to see it full size.
  • Every question starts at full points. Hints and wrong guesses cost you. Chain correct answers for a combo.
